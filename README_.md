## Selected Case Studies

### Case Study 2 — Progressive Electricity Billing
Calculates household electricity bills using progressive consumption slabs, fixed charges, a surcharge, and a qualifying rebate; compares monthly bills and flags unusually large increases.

### Case Study 13 — ATM Transaction Sequence Simulation
Processes deposits and withdrawals in sequence, maintains the account balance, rejects invalid transactions, and summarizes accepted and rejected transactions.

### Case Study 24 — Courier Charge Optimization
Calculates courier charges using parcel weight, zone, delivery speed, and handling needs, then selects the least expensive eligible service.

### Case Study 31 — String-Based Examination Answer Evaluation
Compares student answers with an answer key, counts correct, incorrect, and unanswered responses, applies negative marking, and calculates the final score.

### Case Study 47 — Sports Tournament Ranking System
Tracks match results, team points, goals scored and conceded, and ranks teams by points followed by goal difference.

### Case Study 33 — Campus Registration Identifier Analyzer
Validates student registration identifiers, extracts their components, and reports invalid identifiers based on the sample format and branch codes.

### Case Study 64 — Tuple-Based Course Registration Validation
Uses tuples to represent registrations, detects duplicate student-course entries, and checks course combinations against prerequisite rules.

### Case Study 75 — Integrated Student Record Consistency Checker
Validates student identifiers, names, marks, attendance, and course records; detects duplicate IDs, missing fields, and out-of-range values.

### Case Study 89 — University Hostel Room Allocation
Allocates students according to room capacity, ignores duplicate requests, and reports room occupancy, vacant beds, and unallocated students.

### Case Study 92 — Multi-Event Sports Scheduling and Scoring
Detects overlapping events for the same team, totals team points across events, and generates a final ranking.
